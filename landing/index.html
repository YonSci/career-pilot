What it does

Discovery, explanation, alerts and drafting. All grounded in evidence.

Most job tools rank titles by keywords. Career Pilot compares the actual requirements of each posting with facts you verified from your own CV, and shows its reasoning. Your first search, usually within an hour of signing up, is the moment it pays off.

◎

Continuous discovery

Every few hours it reads ReliefWeb, employer boards, your email alerts from LinkedIn, Devex or UNjobs, and any feed or careers page you add. Duplicates are merged, closed deadlines flagged.

≡

Requirement-level matching

Each posting gets a 0 to 100 relevance score plus a table of requirements marked met, unknown or not met, each pointing to the evidence behind it. Unknown is never rounded up to met.

✦

Why you fit, and what is missing

Strengths written in terms of your real projects, and gaps stated plainly, including eligibility criteria such as citizenship or language that your CV does not document.

➤

Alerts where you are

Matches above your threshold land in your inbox and on Telegram, with Interested and Skip buttons. One message per job or one digest per search; you choose.

✎

Fact-checked application drafts

When you click Prepare application, it drafts a tailored CV, cover letter and screening answers, then an independent review rejects any claim your evidence does not support. Export as DOCX and PDF.

🔒

Private by design

Your own OpenAI key, encrypted at rest. Your workspace is yours alone. Nothing is submitted and no employer is contacted; every draft is checked against your verified facts and reviewed by you before use.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, and it never will. It prepares documents you review and export; you submit them through the employer's official channel. Automation of application portals is deliberately out of scope.

Where do the postings come from?

From sources you enable: ReliefWeb, employer boards on Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, SmartRecruiters and Ashby, Remotive, any RSS feed or public careers page, and job-alert emails from LinkedIn, Devex, UNjobs, Impactpool or ReliefWeb that you route to a mailbox label. LinkedIn itself is never scraped.

What does "bring your own key" mean?

You paste an OpenAI API key in your account. Evaluations and drafts run on it, so you pay OpenAI directly, typically 1 to 3 dollars for a search of 50 postings. The key is stored encrypted and can be removed at any time.

Is the match score a hiring probability?

No. It is a relevance score between the posting's requirements and your verified facts. Mandatory eligibility is tracked separately and shown as met, unknown or not met.

What happens to my data?

Your CV text, verified facts, postings, evaluations and drafts stay in your private workspace. The owner of the service sees only account-level activity to improve the beta. You can remove connections at any time and ask for full deletion. See the privacy notice.
