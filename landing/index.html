What it does

Discovery, explanation, alerts and drafting. All grounded in evidence.

Most job tools rank titles by keywords. Jobs Find AI compares the actual requirements of each posting with facts you verified from your own CV, and shows its reasoning. Your first search, usually within an hour of signing up, is the moment it pays off.

◎

Continuous discovery

Every few hours it reads ReliefWeb, employer boards, your email alerts from LinkedIn, Devex or UNjobs, and any feed or careers page you add. Duplicates are merged, closed deadlines flagged.

≡

Requirement-level matching

Each posting gets a 0 to 100 relevance score plus a table of requirements marked met, unknown or not met, each pointing to the evidence behind it. Unknown is never rounded up to met.

✦

Why you fit, and what is missing

Strengths written in terms of your real projects, and gaps stated plainly, including eligibility criteria such as citizenship or language that your CV does not document.

➤

Alerts where you are

Matches above your threshold land in your inbox and on Telegram, with Interested and Skip buttons. One message per job or one digest per search; you choose.

✎

Evidence-checked application drafts

When you click Prepare application, it drafts a tailored CV, cover letter and screening answers, then an independent review flags any claim your evidence does not support and rewrites it. Export as DOCX and PDF.

🔒

Private by design

Your own OpenAI key, encrypted at rest. Your workspace is yours alone. Nothing is submitted and no employer is contacted; you review every draft before use.

Questions

Straight answers.

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, and it never will. It prepares documents you review and export; you submit them through the employer's official channel. Automation of application portals is deliberately out of scope.

Where do the postings come from?

From sources you enable: ReliefWeb, employer boards on Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, SmartRecruiters and Ashby, Remotive, any RSS feed or public careers page, and job-alert emails from LinkedIn, Devex, UNjobs, Impactpool or ReliefWeb that you route to a mailbox label. LinkedIn itself is never scraped.

What does a search cost me in OpenAI usage?

The first beta members have AI usage included: up to 25 evaluations per search, 300 per month and 5 application packages a month on the founder's key. Everyone else pastes their own OpenAI API key and pays OpenAI directly: a search that evaluates 50 new postings typically costs 1 to 3 US dollars, later searches less because stored postings are not re-evaluated, and drafting one application is usually under a dollar. Members with included AI can add their own key at any time to lift the limits.

Which countries and languages does it cover?

Any country: sources are global and you set your preferred locations, from "Ethiopia" to "Remote" to "Africa". Postings in English are matched best; other languages work when the posting and your CV facts share a language. The assistant and dashboard are in English.

Is the match score a hiring probability?

No. It is a relevance score between the posting's requirements and your verified facts. Mandatory eligibility is tracked separately and shown as met, unknown or not met.

What happens to my data?

Your CV text, verified facts, postings, evaluations and drafts are visible only in your workspace. They are stored on a server the owner administers; running the service, the owner looks only at account-level activity such as whether a CV was verified, not at your documents. API keys and mailbox passwords are stored encrypted. You can remove connections at any time and ask for export or full deletion. See the privacy notice.
