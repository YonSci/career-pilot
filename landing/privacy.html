Privacy notice

Beta service. Last updated 2026-09-20.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, display name, a salted password hash, sign-in sessions.
  • Your career evidence: the CV text you upload or paste, the facts extracted from it, and your verification decisions.
  • Your preferences and sources: keywords, locations, the job feeds and mailbox label you configure.
  • Postings and evaluations: job postings collected from the sources you enable, and the AI's assessment of each one against your verified facts.
  • Application drafts you request, with every version you save.
  • Connection secrets: your OpenAI API key and mailbox (IMAP) credentials, if you add them. They are encrypted at rest and used only to act on your behalf.
  • Telegram: the chat ID of the private chat you link.

How it is used

Only to run the service for you: collecting postings, evaluating them against your evidence, sending you the alerts you enable, and drafting documents you explicitly request. Nothing is submitted to employers. No other member can see your workspace. The service owner administers the server and database where your data is stored; in running the service the owner looks only at account-level activity (for example whether a CV was verified or how many drafts were requested) and does not read your CV contents or drafts except to resolve a support request you make.

Third parties

  • OpenAI processes your verified facts and the postings to produce evaluations and drafts, under your own API key. Requests are sent with storage disabled.
  • Job sources (ReliefWeb, employer boards, feeds, your mailbox) are read on your instruction.
  • Telegram and email providers deliver the alerts you enable.
  • The hosting provider stores the encrypted database on infrastructure in the EU.

Your control

You can remove your API key, mailbox credentials and Telegram link at any time under Account. To export or delete your whole workspace, contact the service owner at the address you received your invitation from; deletion is completed within 14 days.

Retention

Postings that disappear from feeds and were never shortlisted are archived after 45 days. Accounts inactive for 12 months may be deleted after notice.
